# 19 — Open questions

These require product, editorial or business input; they are not unresolved merely because implementation detail remains.

1. Which desks and editorial roles exist at launch, and who may approve/fact-check/correct each desk’s work?
2. Is advertising part of the first cutover? If yes, which current Advertiser/Campaign/Ad/Event behaviors and retention obligations must survive?
3. What are the canonical public locale policies for English and Bengali, including whether localized slugs may diverge?
4. What correction display policy is required: inline notice, correction history page, or both?
5. Which source types may be ingested automatically, and what copyright/terms/robots policy governs retrieval and raw snapshot retention?
6. Which contributor contact fields may migrate and who can view them?
7. What publishing SLA, RPO/RTO, traffic baseline and peak-event assumptions should set capacity targets?
8. Which OIDC provider constraints apply: managed tenancy, self-hosting, region, MFA, directory integration and lifecycle automation?
9. Which distribution channels are launch requirements versus later experiments?
10. What analytics consent, retention and newsroom access policy applies to public readers and internal users?
