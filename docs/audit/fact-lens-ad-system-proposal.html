Ad System Proposal

5 October 2026 · Prepared for tajmin · Scope: homepage first, with the rules every page uses. Approved 5 October 2026; updated 6 October 2026 for wireframe V2 and the scope audit (web) and V1 (mobile). Horizontal ads between sections are full-viewport bands; ads are the main revenue source; launch ads are static images from closed networks.

Use five standard ad slots with multi-size requests, reserve their space per breakpoint, and keep the page inside the Better Ads Standards. Mobile is about three quarters of human traffic in Bangladesh, so the phone slots carry most of the revenue.

Recommendation in short.
  1. “Full width” applies to the band: background spans the viewport; the row has no padding, margin or gutter. The creative inside is a standard IAB size, centered in the shell. Buyers sell fixed sizes; no programmatic demand exists for a 1920px banner.
  2. Five slots: ad-band-top, ad-band, ad-inset (between rows inside a section, new 6 October), ad-rail (article pages only, later) and ad-inline (articles, later). No ad is sticky or fixed at any width, so there is no mobile anchor (6 October).
  3. Ads have no padding, margin or gutter at any width (6 October). An ad row is the creative height plus the label line. The editorial sections keep their own spacing next to it.
  4. Phase 1 (launch): static image ads from closed networks. Each slot shows one creative per tier at a standard IAB size. Phase 2 (later): Google Ad Manager or another network fills the same slots with multi-size requests. Google: “The more sizes you specify when defining a slot, the more ads that will be eligible to serve to it.”
  5. The top band sits above the masthead (your decision, 6 October; it replaces “below the navigation”). It scrolls away; the top bar sticks after it. From 1024px it shows a 970×250 with no padding, as on reuters.com (L-28).
  6. Reserve each slot’s height in CSS per breakpoint (creative height + 24px label line), and lazy-load everything below the first screen. No timed refresh in phase 1.

Rules for the layout doc

Written as STE rules for the rewrite. IDs are provisional.

  1. Label every slot “Advertisement” (EN) or “বিজ্ঞাপন” (BN) above the creative, in type-eyebrow and ink-muted.
  2. Put every slot between two sections in an ad band. The band background spans the viewport. The label and creative stay in the shell. Put a slot between two rows inside a section in the wrapper (ad-inset), with no band. Ad rows and insets have no padding, margin or gutter at any width; the editorial sections keep space-module next to them.
  3. Reserve the creative height in CSS (min-height) for each tier. Do not set the height in JavaScript.
  4. Request all sizes listed for the slot and tier. Map tiers to the agreed breakpoints with GPT size mapping.
  5. Load ad-band-top first. Lazy-load all other slots. Phase 1: native image lazy loading. Phase 2: GPT lazy load, fetch at 5 viewports, render at 2, double both on mobile (Google’s example values).
  6. Keep ad density under 30% of the main content height on every device. This is the Better Ads limit for phones and stricter than its 50% desktop limit.
  7. Phase 1: do not refresh. Rotate bookings per page load. Phase 2: refresh only a viewable slot, no more than once every 30 seconds, and declare it in Ad Manager.
  8. Do not use pop-ups, prestitials, countdown postitials, scrollovers, flashing creatives or video that plays sound on its own.
  9. Do not make any ad sticky or fixed, at any width. Ads scroll with the page.
  10. Use ad-rail on article pages only (later). Do not put a rail ad on the homepage.
  11. When ad-band-top has no ad, show a house ad (newsletter or app). When another slot has no ad before it nears the viewport, collapse the whole band, label included.
  12. Do not put an ad inside a peer row, a story list or an article block. Ads never look like story cards, and editorial boxes (newsletter, poll) never look like ads.
  13. Put at least 600px of editorial content between two ad slots on phones. Show no more than 3 ad-inset slots on a page.
  14. Put ad-band-top as the first row of the page, above the header. Show 970×250 from 1024px. The top bar is the next row and sticks at the top after the band scrolls away.
  15. Put an ad-band after the hero, after Opinion and analysis, after each featured category and after Most read. Put an ad-inset between two rows of grouped categories, never after the last row.

Phase 1: static ads from closed networks

At launch, ads are static images that Fact Lens or a closed network books directly. The slots, sizes and reserved heights stay the same, so a programmatic network can fill them later with no layout change.

  • Creative set per booking. The advertiser supplies one image per tier: 970×250 (or 728×90) for 1024+, 728×90 for 768–1023, and 320×100 or 300×250 for phones. An ad-inset booking needs 728×90 and 300×250. Ask for 2× files for sharp screens.
  • Markup. One <picture> with a <source> per tier, width and height on the image, and the same reserved height as the slot. The browser loads only the file for the current tier.
  • Files. WebP or AVIF with a JPEG/PNG fallback. Keep the IAB weight limits: 150 kB for 300×250 and 728×90, 250 kB for 970×250, 50 kB for 320×50.
  • Link. The image links to the advertiser with rel="sponsored noopener" and target="_blank". Give it alt text that names the advertiser.
  • Rotation. When a slot has more than one booking, pick one per page load. No timed refresh.
  • Counting. Count impressions when 50% of the ad is in view for 1 second (the MRC viewability standard), and count clicks. Advertisers in a closed network still expect these reports.
  • Empty slot. With no booking, ad-band-top shows a house ad; other bands collapse (AD-11).
  • Data source. In the prototype, ads come from mock JSON like the stories. Each entry has slot, advertiser, link, dates and one image per tier.

Decisions (approved 5 October, updated 6 October 2026)

1. Top band position. Above the masthead decided 6 Oct. 970×250 from 1024px with no padding, as on reuters.com (AD-14, L-28, L-32).
2. Sticky ads. None at any width decided 6 Oct. The mobile anchor (320×50) and the sticky rail ad are removed (AD-09).
3. Homepage rail ad. None decided 6 Oct. Rail ads may go on article pages later (AD-10, L-35).
4. Homepage bands. The V2 placement decided 6 Oct: top band, a band after the hero, Opinion, each featured category and Most read (AD-15).
5. Ad spacing. No padding, margin or gutter on ads at any width decided 6 Oct. The ad row is the creative height plus a 24px label line (AD-01, AD-02).
6. Ad between category rows. decided 6 Oct New slot ad-inset: wrapper width, 728×90 from 1280px (reserve 90), 300×250 below, phones too (reserve 250), at most 3 per page (AD-02, AD-13, L-33, L-34).
7. Ad label line. decided 6 Oct The label line is 24px (1.5rem), so the Bangla label (type-eyebrow, about 20px tall) fits and the reserved height holds. The top band is 274px (S-12, layout v2.1).
8. Band surface. decided 6 Oct Ad rows and the ad-inset box use surface-alt; the label uses ink-muted. The Opinion and analysis section gets its own full-width background, different from the page and from the ad rows (token to be defined).

Ad stack (for the tech decision)

  • Phase 2: Google Ad Manager with Google Publisher Tag. AdSense and Ad Exchange demand compete in it. Direct-sold Bangladeshi campaigns go in the same slots.
  • Single Request Architecture: define all slots in the document head, then call display().
  • Header bidding (Prebid) can come after launch, when traffic justifies it. The slot design above does not change for it.
  • The prototype uses placeholder boxes at the reserved sizes. Real ad code comes with the backend milestone.
  • Revenue numbers (RPM, fill) are not estimated here. Measure them after launch and tune the reserved sizes and band count from Ad Manager reports.
